## Notable milestone-time decision

The agent-state signal was **redesigned at the human-verify gate**: the originally-planned reused `StatusDot` (dot) sitting beside the CI glyph read as two clashing marks, so the user chose to drop the dot and carry the full agent state on an always-on colored **left rail** (green working / pulsing-amber waiting / blue idle / gray exited). This splits the two signals onto separate channels (left edge = your agent, right glyph = the PR's CI). The design contract (16-CONTEXT.md D-04/06/07, 16-UI-SPEC.md) and 16-02-SUMMARY.md were revised to match.
